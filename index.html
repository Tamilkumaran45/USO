About Unified Sports Organization

About this app

Unified Sports Organization is a complete cricket tournament management platform built for colleges, schools, and local organizers. It simplifies team registration, fixture creation, score updates, and leaderboard management.

Designed for smooth coordination between admins, coaches, and players, the platform ensures a structured and efficient tournament experience.

Highlights

Team Management
Register teams, manage rosters, and handle player roles easily.
Fixture Scheduling
Auto-generate match schedules and update venues instantly.
Live Score Updates
Real-time scoring, player stats, and match progress tracking.
Points Table
Automatic points and standings calculation for every match.

Technical & Privacy

Built using PHP & MySQL, Unified Sports Organization provides secure performance and reliable data handling. The application prioritizes accuracy, transparency, and seamless tournament workflows.
